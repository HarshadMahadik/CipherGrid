Troubleshooting & FAQ

Solutions for common developer questions and customization scenarios.

Q: Are images and fonts loaded from external CDNs?

No. All vendor stylesheets (Bootstrap 5.3.3, Bootstrap Icons) and web fonts (WOFF2/WOFF) are stored locally in the vendor/ directory. CipherGrid is 100% functional offline without internet access.

Q: Does CipherGrid require Node.js, npm, or Webpack to edit?

No. CipherGrid is intentionally built as framework-free standard HTML5, CSS3, and modern ES6+ JavaScript. You can edit any HTML or CSS file directly in VS Code, Sublime Text, or Notepad and see your changes immediately.

Q: Can I use this template for commercial client projects?

Yes! The template includes a commercial license suitable for deploying client websites, SaaS marketing sites, corporate portals, and cybersecurity agency landing pages.
